# V1 Matching Weights

Suggested starting weights:
- topic fit: 30%
- skill fit: 20%
- method fit: 15%
- advisor fit: 15%
- location fit: 10%
- funding fit: 5%
- competitiveness fit: 5%

## Tiering
- 90-100: strong match
- 75-89: good match
- 60-74: stretch match
- below 60: exclude by default

## Rule
Ranking must stay deterministic until enough feedback exists to justify learning-based tuning.

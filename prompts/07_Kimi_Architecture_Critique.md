# Kimi 2.5 Prompt — Architecture Critique

You are acting as a Principal Software Architect reviewing a startup's backend design.

Your job is to criticize the design.

Assume this platform must eventually support:
- 1 million users
- millions of opportunities
- millions of supervisors
- multiple AI models
- international expansion

Your task:
Find:
- architectural weaknesses
- scaling bottlenecks
- database problems
- poor abstractions
- technical debt risks
- security risks
- missing modules
- future problems

For every issue:
- explain severity
- explain why it matters
- explain how to improve

Do NOT rewrite the whole architecture.
Review it like a senior engineer.

# OpenCode Prompt — Opportunity Database

Design the database schema for storing academic opportunities.

Requirements:
- support PhD
- support Postdoc
- support Master
- support Research Assistant
- support Faculty
- support Industry Research

Every opportunity must have:
- institution
- department
- funding
- supervisor
- methods
- skills
- topics
- deadline
- source
- URL
- country
- language
- confidence

Use SQLAlchemy.

Produce:
- ER diagram
- models
- indexes
- migration strategy

Do not implement.
Only design.

# Project Overview

## Mission
Help researchers and highly skilled professionals discover the career opportunity where they are most likely to succeed.

## Product promise
The platform should not only show opportunities. It should explain which opportunities fit the user, why they fit, and what the user should do next.

## First launch philosophy
Start small:
- profile understanding
- supervisor and opportunity matching
- explanation
- email digest
- shortlist tracking

## What the product is not, at least at first
- not a full social network
- not a large marketplace
- not a generic search engine
- not a broad job board clone

## Design principle
Optimize for fit quality, trust, and clarity, not for the maximum number of search results.
